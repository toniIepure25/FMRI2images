## Interpretation

- V62a validation is the easiest and strongest visible 768-D CLIP manifold protocol.
- V62a validation NeighborhoodOverlap@10 is about 514x over the analytic random baseline, which strongly supports local semantic neighborhood preservation on that split.
- V62a SHARED1000 target-embeddings 1k gallery is the direct benchmark-comparable protocol because `shared1000_metrics.json` reports `gallery_size=1000` over saved SHARED1000 targets.
- Critical finding: `shared1000_metrics.json` is reproduced exactly for cosine when `gallery=shared1000_ground_truth.npy` and `target_indices=np.arange(1000)`.
- The previous `20260514_V62a_shared1000_1k_gallery_hardened` run is diagnostic: its R@K values are far below `shared1000_metrics.json`.
- V62a SHARED1000 full 10k gallery is valid after ID mapping, but harder and different; it should be reported as "1000 shared queries over a 10k gallery".
- V61a MC-TTA token-space analysis should be kept separate from 768-D CLIP analysis. It is useful evidence that CSLS improves ranking and reduces hubness in a harder token-space protocol.
- Fusion remains rank/score-level unless a true fused 768-D embedding export is created. Geometry, RSA, and neighborhood claims for fusion remain unavailable.
