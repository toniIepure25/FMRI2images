# Phase 19 — Target journal profiles

Three profiles by fit, **not** prestige. No acceptance-probability claims. Any specific journal name, scope, or
current policy below should be **verified from the journal's official site by the human authors before
submission**; the descriptions are generic-category expectations, not quoted policy.

## Profile A — Computational / systems neuroscience
- **Audience fit:** readers interested in representational geometry, perception–imagery transformations, and
  subspace/manifold framings. Strong fit for the conceptual decomposition and the geometric predictor.
- **Novelty expectation:** high conceptual novelty; will scrutinise the delta over the transformation and
  hyperalignment literatures. The novelty sentence must be airtight.
- **Replication expectation:** moderate; a preregistered-but-pending replication is acceptable if framed as
  discovery + prospective plan.
- **Likely reviewer concerns:** neural meaning of constructs; N = 8; subject-specificity interpretation.
- **Length/style:** conceptual framing forward; methods can be substantial; supplement carries the gate ledger.
- **Representative venues (verify scope/policy):** systems/computational neuroscience journals and
  neuroscience-methods-friendly society journals.

## Profile B — Neuroimaging / methodological
- **Audience fit:** readers focused on fMRI decoding, calibration/few-shot methods, reliability, and
  reproducibility. Strong fit for the minimal-calibration frontier, schedule-robustness audit, and the
  provenance/reproducibility engineering.
- **Novelty expectation:** methodological novelty (calibration frontier + fragility + geometric predictor)
  weighs as much as conceptual novelty.
- **Replication expectation:** high emphasis on reproducibility; the frozen pipeline + preregistration are a
  strength here.
- **Likely reviewer concerns:** beta/voxel-selection disclosure; estimator specificity; N = 8.
- **Length/style:** methods-heavy; detailed supplement welcomed.
- **Representative venues (verify):** neuroimaging methods journals.

## Profile C — Interdisciplinary neuroscience
- **Audience fit:** broad readership; requires a crisp, accessible take-home and strong framing.
- **Novelty expectation:** high bar for general significance; the "development cohort + pending replication"
  status may be a harder sell without an independent cohort.
- **Replication expectation:** often high; a pending replication may be a limitation for the most selective
  interdisciplinary venues.
- **Likely reviewer concerns:** generality from N = 8; whether the advance is broad enough.
- **Length/style:** tight main text, extensive supplement.
- **Representative venues (verify):** interdisciplinary neuroscience journals.

## Recommendation (internal)
**Profile B (neuroimaging/methodological)** is the most natural first target: it values the calibration/
reproducibility contribution, tolerates a development-cohort + preregistered-replication framing, and its
reviewers will credit the provenance engineering. Profile A is a strong alternative if the conceptual novelty
sentence survives the human literature check. Defer Profile C until an independent cohort exists.
