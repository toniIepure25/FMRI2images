# MINDIR discovery manuscript — title candidates

All candidates avoid the forbidden terms (universal, law, causal, mechanism, brain computation,
gauge symmetry) and describe the historical cohort honestly as a discovery/development cohort.

1. **Subject-specific geometry and calibration limits in human visual imagery** *(recommended — the preferred conceptual direction; precise and defensible)*
2. **Human visual imagery combines subject-specific target-state geometry with low-complexity calibration** *(stronger form; wording remains justified by O2.6/O2.9/O2.10)*
3. **How much of imagery does perception predict? Subject-specific target-state geometry and minimal direct calibration in an fMRI discovery cohort**
4. **Perception incompletely determines imagery geometry: a minimal target-state calibration frontier and its acquisition fragility**
5. **Repeat-to-repeat geometric agreement predicts calibration generalization in human visual imagery (N = 8 discovery cohort)**

**Recommendation:** Title 1 for a conservative supervisor/preprint submission; Title 2 if a slightly stronger
framing is wanted and the reviewers accept "low-complexity" (supported: the missing target-state basis is
low-dimensional per O2.6, and calibratable from 8 observations per O2.9). Titles 3–5 foreground specific
findings and are better as section/one-line framings than as the primary title.

**Do not** upgrade any title to imply completed replication, a universal minimum, or a causal/mechanistic claim.
