# MRI safety workflow

> **SITE_MEDICAL_POLICY_GOVERNS.** No approval (PI, ethics, site, data-protection, consent, stimulus licence) has been granted. This is a draft to be transferred into the institution's official forms.


We do **not** author a final medical screening instrument; the **site-approved MRI screening form governs**
(`SITE_MEDICAL_POLICY_GOVERNS`). Workflow:

1. Site-approved MRI safety questionnaire completed and reviewed by qualified staff.
2. Screen for: metallic implants; electronic/active implants (pacemakers, neurostimulators); metallic foreign
   bodies; prior surgery; claustrophobia; pregnancy (per site policy); other site-defined contraindications.
3. Hearing protection provided; emergency squeeze-ball and stop procedure explained.
4. Emergency procedures per site policy; operator present throughout.
5. If the **7T** site is selected, apply 7T-specific screening and safety constraints per that facility.

Final medical eligibility and contraindication criteria: `SITE_MEDICAL_POLICY_GOVERNS`.
