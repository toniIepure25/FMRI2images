# First-contact strategy

Do not email everyone at once. Sequence by fit and by what each person can unblock.

## WAVE 1 (2-3 highest-fit; send first)
- **Frank Scharnowski (UniWien)** — why now: strongest method+MRI-access+supervision fit at UniWien. Send:
  PI scientific bundle. Ask: scientific feedback + willingness to supervise/host an independent replication.
  Progress = a meeting.
- **Christian Windischberger (MedUni HFMRC)** — why now: fMRI-methods PI with direct high-field MRI access.
  Send: PI scientific bundle + one line noting the MRI-site brief is available. Ask: feedback + feasibility of
  hosting/advising. Progress = a meeting or a referral to the right site contact.

## WAVE 2 (only if Wave 1 stalls)
- **Claus Lamm (UniWien MR Center director)** and **Ulrich Ansorge (visual cognition)** — send after ~5-7
  working days if no Wave-1 progress. Lamm for facility+supervision; Ansorge for scientific framing/thesis.

## WAVE 3 (technical escalation, after a PI is interested)
- **Ronald Sladky (MR Center manager)** and the institutional site contacts — send the MRI-site bundle only
  once a PI supports the project.

For each contact: WHY NOW / WHAT TO SEND / WHAT TO ASK / WHAT COUNTS AS PROGRESS are as above. A reply agreeing
to a short meeting is the primary progress signal; a substantive scientific critique is also progress.
