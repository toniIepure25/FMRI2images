# Independent replication — one-page plan (frozen, not yet executed)

> This study is **prospectively frozen and preregistered** but has **not** been run; no independent participant
> has been scanned. It is described here as future work.

## Target cohort
- **Planned:** N = 12 participants.
- **Minimum confirmatory valid:** N ≥ 8.
- **Genuinely independent** of the historical NSD participants (no overlap).

## Primary purpose
Independently test the frozen minimal-calibration / generalisation pipeline developed on the discovery cohort —
i.e., whether a small, direct participant-specific imagery calibration recovers the outside-of-perception
target-state geometry, and whether an operational calibration-quality monitor works — in people who contributed
nothing to the discovery analyses.

## Primary analysis (plain language)
Using the exact frozen procedure: build each participant's perception-support geometry and calibration on
training data; freeze and hash that stage before any held-out outcome is opened; then open the held-out data and
evaluate calibration recovery, its operational sufficiency against the pre-registered criterion, acquisition-
schedule robustness, and the participant-level association between repeat geometry and calibration success. The
primary result is decided by a pre-declared, multiplicity-controlled test family and is frozen before any
secondary analysis is examined.

## Secondary preregistered endpoints (geometric)
Three secondary hypotheses, each carried across two regions, evaluated **only after** the primary result is
frozen:
- **S1 — principal-angle invariant:** does the shared perception↔imagery angular relationship replicate?
- **S2 — repeat-geometry → calibration robustness:** does outside-of-perception repeat agreement predict the
  calibration margin?
- **S3 — geometric anisotropy:** are repeat-level subspace perturbations reproducibly anisotropic beyond a
  matched isotropic baseline?

## Firewall
The secondary endpoints **cannot rescue or modify the primary result**. A secondary positive does not save a
failed primary replication; a secondary negative does not erase a successful one. No post-hoc reinterpretation.

## Interpretation, either way
- **If the primary replicates:** supports external generalisation of the minimal-calibration result and its
  operational quality monitor beyond the discovery cohort.
- **If the primary fails:** the discovery-cohort calibration result does not generalise under the frozen
  protocol — a clean, preregistered outcome.

## Proposed acquisition (targets, not commitments)
- **Perception:** 512 canonical anchor images × 3 presentations.
- **Imagery:** 12 identities × 8 repeats.
- **Scanner target:** 7T where feasible; ~1.8 mm isotropic functional; TR ~1.6 s; T1 ≤ 1 mm; T2 recommended.

**Unresolved (must not be presented as settled):** the actual scanner/site is **not selected**; the full
session/run structure is **not fully certified**; stimulus permissions are **unresolved** where applicable;
ethics approval is **not obtained**; and the real-site BIDS implementation is **not yet validated**. No scanner
access is implied to exist.
