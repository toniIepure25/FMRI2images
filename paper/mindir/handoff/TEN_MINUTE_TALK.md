# Ten-minute talk outline (≤ 8 slides)

*Development cohort N = 8. Independent replication is future work. No hype.*

**Slide 1 — Question (~1 min)**
Imagery resembles perception but is not identical. Which components of the imagined ("target") neural state does
perception fail to specify, are they shared across people, and how little direct imagery data recovers them?
Public NSD data; eight participants; a frozen, pre-registered pipeline; participant = unit of inference.

**Slide 2 — Perception-supported vs imagery-specific geometry (~1.5 min)**
Split the imagined state relative to a perception-support subspace. A substantial part is perception-supported,
but a real, low-dimensional residual lies outside it — the perception ceiling sits below a within-participant
oracle. *Takeaway: imagery is not a replay of perception geometry.*

**Slide 3 — Subject specificity (~1.5 min)**
The residual's orientation does not transfer across people, and is not predicted by the tested perception
phenotype, anatomy, or behaviour. *Takeaway: subject-specific under the tested representation and transfer —
not a claim of biological uniqueness.*

**Slide 4 — Minimal calibration frontier (~1.5 min)**
Sweep imagined-identities × repeats under a fixed estimator. Eight observations (four identities × two repeats)
was the smallest prospectively tested common burden recovering ≥ half the gap in both streams (ventral 0.569,
lateral 0.530). *Takeaway: cheap calibration is possible — but this is not a universal minimum.*

**Slide 5 — Schedule fragility and the geometric predictor (~1.5 min)**
Holding eight observations fixed, coverage was only 0.656 / 0.579, with no schedule-robust minimum. Repeat-to-
repeat outside-of-perception geometric agreement predicted calibration success (median ρ 0.423 / 0.477; 8/8
positive). *Takeaway: which repeats you acquire matters, not just how many.*

**Slide 6 — Exploratory mechanism constraints (~1 min)**
*(Exploratory, same N = 8.)* One shared angular invariant; no discrete latent states; no scalar-reliability
explanation; anisotropic repeat perturbations whose dominant mode does not predict failure. *Takeaway:
structured but distributed.*

**Slide 7 — Independent replication (~1 min)**
Prospectively frozen, preregistered; planned N = 12 (minimum 8), independent cohort; primary generalisation test
plus three preregistered secondary geometric endpoints that cannot modify the primary. **Not yet executed.**

**Slide 8 — Ask / next step (~0.5 min)**
Seeking scientific feedback, a PI to evaluate the replication, MRI access, and ethics guidance. Coauthorship
only where scientifically warranted. Next step is human feedback before anything is run.
