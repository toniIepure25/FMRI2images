# MRI safety & eligibility screening checklist — DRAFT

> DRAFT support tool. The **authoritative** MRI-safety screening is the institution's approved form; this does not replace it. Complete institution-specific version before any scanning.

- [ ] Adult (age per institutional policy)
- [ ] Informed consent completed (approved version)
- [ ] MRI-safe: no non-removable ferromagnetic implants/devices (pacemaker, aneurysm clips, cochlear implant, certain stents, metal fragments, etc.) — per institutional MRI-safety form
- [ ] Not pregnant / per institutional policy
- [ ] No contraindicated conditions (claustrophobia severity, weight/bore limits) — per institution
- [ ] Normal or corrected-to-normal vision (MRI-compatible correction available)
- [ ] Able to understand and perform perception + imagery tasks
- [ ] **No overlap with the development cohort (NSD subj01–08)** — independence requirement
- [ ] Pseudonymous ID assigned (REP-NNN); identity mapping stored securely & separately
- [ ] Institutional MRI-safety officer sign-off: [PLACEHOLDER]
