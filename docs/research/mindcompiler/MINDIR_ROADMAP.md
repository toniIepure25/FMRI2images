# MINDIR research roadmap

Each stage has explicit entry/exit criteria. Synthetic work may advance software/identifiability/feasibility;
only new participants advance scientific status.

## R0 — Historical discovery — **CLOSED**
- Entry: development cohort (N=8). Exit: sealed O2–X4 findings; discovery permanently closed. **DONE.**

## R1 — Independent replication — **CURRENT**
- Entry: frozen O2.16 primary + O2.16-SEC + engineering stack + site prep + authorization package (all done).
- Exit: a genuinely independent cohort acquired; O2.16 primary executed and sealed; O2.16-SEC executed and sealed.
- Blockers: PI, MRI site, ethics, consent, stimulus permissions (all human).

## R2 — Moonshot development on Cohort B
- Entry: R1 exit (Cohort-B outcomes unlocked). Exit: M1/M2/M3 candidates evaluated prospectively on Cohort B
  under their preregistered rules. Any candidate → Cohort-C template.

## R3 — Cohort-C confirmation
- Entry: an R2 candidate meeting its support rule. Exit: independent Cohort C (disjoint from A and B) confirms or
  refutes it prospectively. No post-hoc re-fitting.

## R4 — Cross-session / cross-state validation
- Entry: R3 for the relevant finding. Exit: E1 (fingerprint), E3 (cross-session zero-shot), E4 (cross-state)
  validated on independent data.

## R5 — Multi-site / generalization
- Entry: R4. Exit: E6 (3T/7T or two facilities) separates representation-level from site-dependent quantities.

## R6 — General personalized internal-state framework
- Entry: R4–R5 convergent evidence. Exit: a validated, documented framework for personalized internal-state
  geometry with behavioral relevance (E5) and cross-representation robustness (E10). **Aspirational.**

## Invariants across all stages
No reopening of historical N=8; no retrospective threshold tuning; no outcome peeking; participant is the
inferential unit; every discovery requires independent confirmation; O3 stays `O3_NOT_READY` until its own
prerequisites (not addressed here) are met.
