# MSc thesis paths (`MSC_THESIS_PATH.md`)

Five viable thesis cores. **No winner recommended** — the choice depends on MRI access, sessions, and PI steer.

| Core | Scientific question | Required data | Scope | Risk | Expected duration | MRI-access dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Independent replication | Do the development findings replicate? | 1 independent cohort (N=12, min 8) | Focused, high-rigor | Moderate (null is publishable) | ~1 acquisition + analysis term | HIGH |
| B. Adaptive calibration | Can we know online when enough imagery data is collected? | Cohort B + shadow policy | Method + acquisition | Moderate | + shadow analysis | HIGH |
| C. Zero-shot subject geometry | Predict imagery geometry from non-imagery data? | Cohort B (rich descriptors) | Ambitious | Higher (N~12 power) | HIGH |
| D. Cross-session fingerprint | Is private geometry a stable subject trait? | ≥2 sessions/participant | Novel | Moderate-high | HIGH + repeated sessions |
| E. Cross-state delayed recall | Same structure in recall as imagery? | Cohort B + recall block | Novel | Higher (ethics/scope) | HIGH + extra block |

All cores keep the frozen replication as the backbone; B-E are extensions that require the corresponding
acquisition. A is the lowest-risk, self-contained thesis; D/E are the highest-upside.
