# Study protocol — Neural representations of visual perception and mental imagery

> **DRAFT_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW.** No approval (PI, ethics, site, data-protection, consent, stimulus licence) has been granted. This is a draft to be transferred into the institution's official forms.


## Title
Neural Representations of Visual Perception and Mental Imagery (independent replication study).

## Background
A prior **development cohort** (N = 8, a public dataset) was used to develop analysis methods relating visual
perception and mental imagery neural representations. Further analysis of that development cohort is closed. This
study is a genuinely **independent prospective replication** in newly recruited participants, with no overlap
with the development cohort.

## Scientific question
Do the perception→imagery calibration and generalization findings from the development cohort replicate in an
independent cohort, and do preregistered secondary geometric endpoints hold?

## Rationale
Independent replication in new participants is required before any development-cohort finding can be considered
general. The analysis is prospectively frozen; outcomes are not used to change the design.

## Primary objective
Execute the frozen primary replication analysis (fixed estimator, fixed operational criterion, six folds) on the
independent cohort and seal the result.

## Secondary objectives
Evaluate the three preregistered secondary geometric endpoints (participant-level), reported separately and
unable to modify the primary result.

## Study design
Prospective, single-arm, within-participant fMRI acquisition (perception task + imagery task) with a frozen,
preregistered analysis pipeline and a Stage-A/Stage-B outcome firewall.

## Participants
Healthy adult volunteers able to undergo MRI and understand the task language. Planned N = 12; minimum
analyzable N ≥ 8. No development-cohort overlap.

## Sample size
Planned N = 12 (minimum confirmatory-valid N ≥ 8). Participant is the inferential unit; power for small effects
is limited, which is why an independent confirmation cohort is required for any exploratory follow-up.

## Acquisition
Whole-brain functional MRI (targets: ~1.8 mm isotropic, TR ~1.6 s), structural T1w (+ T2w). Exact sequence,
field strength, run/session partition and hardware are **SITE-TBD** (see the site decision register).

## Tasks
Perception task (viewing a fixed set of anchor images, multiple presentations) and a cued mental-imagery task
(imagining a fixed identity set, several repeats). Trial counts are frozen; run partition is site-reviewable.

## Outcomes
Primary: the frozen operational replication criterion. Secondary: the three preregistered geometric endpoints.
No new outcome is defined here.

## Blinding / outcome firewall
Predictor-side (Stage-A) processing is frozen and hashed before any held-out outcome (Stage-B) is opened; a
human analysis custodian controls the Stage-B release. No outcome peeking.

## Data management
Pseudonymous IDs only in the research dataset; identity mapping held separately (see data-protection plan).

## Risks / benefits
Standard MRI risks (see MRI safety workflow); no guaranteed individual benefit. Scientific benefit: understanding
neural representations of perception and imagery.
