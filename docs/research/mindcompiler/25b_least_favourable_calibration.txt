TWO FINDINGS THAT TRANSFER:

1. W1c behaves as predicted. Incomplete observed features yield a POSITIVE mean
   incremental source value (+0.017) with NO state transformation present. Since
   no real feature battery is exhaustive, a positive real-data result is always
   compatible with W1c. This is why NSD-Imagery reaches Level B at best.

2. W1f is far worse than anticipated and is the binding constraint. A single
   unobserved condition-level attentional/nuisance variable shared by both states
   produces mean dR2 = +0.470 -- larger than the genuine transformation's +0.213.
   Calibrating honestly against the least-favourable null therefore sets the
   threshold ABOVE the alternative, and POWER COLLAPSES TO ZERO.

CONSEQUENCE: under least-favourable calibration at this configuration the
detector is valid (max FPR 0.000) but USELESS (power 0.00). An honest E-M1
cannot distinguish a genuine condition-level transformation from a shared
unobserved nuisance at n_id=12 unless the nuisance is bounded by design or
measurement. This is a design finding, not a tuning problem: it says the
attentional/nuisance confound must be MEASURED (or excluded by design), not
assumed away.

W1f's strength is a free parameter chosen here; its exact magnitude is not a
claim about real data. What is claimed: an unobserved shared condition-level
nuisance is a devastating null for this estimand, and it must be addressed
before any Level B interpretation.
