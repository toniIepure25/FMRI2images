# Participant information sheet

> **DRAFT_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW.** No approval (PI, ethics, site, data-protection, consent, stimulus licence) has been granted. This is a draft to be transferred into the institution's official forms.


*Plain-language; educated non-specialist. No internal study jargon.*

**What is this study about?** We are studying how the brain represents what people see and what they picture in
their mind's eye, using magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).

**What would I do?** In an MRI scanner you would (1) look at a series of images, and (2) imagine specific items
when prompted. There are short breaks. The approximate time will be confirmed before you take part (it depends on
the final scanner protocol).

**Is it voluntary?** Yes. Taking part is entirely **voluntary**. You may **withdraw at any time, without giving a
reason and without any disadvantage**.

**Are there risks?** MRI is generally safe for eligible people but involves a strong magnetic field and loud
noise (hearing protection is provided). You will complete an MRI safety screening. People with certain implants
or conditions cannot be scanned.

**Will it help me?** There is **no guaranteed personal or medical benefit** to you. This is research, not a
medical examination, and is not designed to diagnose any condition.

**What happens to my data?** Your research data are stored under a **pseudonymous code**, not your name. Your
identity information is kept separately and securely. Only aggregate/scientific findings are published.
