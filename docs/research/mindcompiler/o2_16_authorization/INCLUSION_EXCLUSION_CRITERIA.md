# Inclusion / exclusion criteria

> **DRAFT_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW.** No approval (PI, ethics, site, data-protection, consent, stimulus licence) has been granted. This is a draft to be transferred into the institution's official forms.


## Inclusion
- Adult (age threshold per site/ethics).
- Normal or corrected-to-normal vision.
- Able to understand the task-language instructions.
- Able and eligible to undergo MRI.

## Exclusion — MRI SAFETY
- Any site-defined MRI contraindication (`SITE_MEDICAL_POLICY_GOVERNS`).

## Exclusion — SCIENTIFIC (prospectively defined)
- Overlap with the historical development cohort.
- Inability to perform the imagery task after standardized instruction/practice (assessed before scanning).

## Exclusion — DATA-QUALITY (prospectively defined, outcome-independent)
- Excessive head motion or acquisition failure per **pre-registered** thresholds, defined before any outcome.

**Never** exclude based on poor scientific outcome, low model performance, or an undesirable replication result.
